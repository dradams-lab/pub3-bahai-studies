# Mahabbat and the Two Wings
### Quantum Physics as Structural Convergence with Bahá'í Relational Ontology

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/doi/10.5281/zenodo.23046022.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23046022)

**Author:** Joshua Adams  
**Target journal:** Journal of Bahá'í Studies  
**Status:** Preprint

---

This paper tests the Bahá'í two-wings principle against three landmarks of modern physics — Bell's theorem, relational quantum mechanics, and the experiments recognized by the 2022 Nobel Prize — and argues that the Bahá'í metaphysics of *mahabbat* (relational love as the creative foundation of existence) shares a logical structure with relational quantum mechanics and Whitehead's process philosophy: existence constituted by relationship, and properties emerging through relational interaction. The further claim, that what sustains relationship is love, belongs to the Bahá'í writings (and, in its own vocabulary, to Whitehead), not to physics.
