# Mahabbat and the Affinity Tensor
### Quantum Physics as Structural Convergence with Bahá'í Relational Ontology

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/doi/10.5281/zenodo.20388678.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20388678)

**Author:** Joshua Adams  
**Target journal:** Journal of Bahá'í Studies  
**Status:** Preprint

---

This paper tests the Bahá'í two-wings principle against the deepest findings of modern physics — Bell's theorem, relational quantum mechanics, and the 2022 Nobel Prize results — and argues that the Bahá'í metaphysics of *mahabbat* (relational love as the creative foundation of existence) is structurally isomorphic to relational quantum mechanics and Whitehead's process philosophy.
