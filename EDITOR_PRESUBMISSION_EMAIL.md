The paper is titled **"Mahabbat and the Affinity Operator: Quantum Physics as
Structural Convergence with Bahá'í Relational Ontology"** and I intend to submit it
to the Articles section. I used one AI tool — Claude, by Anthropic — in three
distinct ways:

1. **Drafting (mixed by section).** The core argument — the textual case for the
   Bahá'í relational metaphysics of *mahabbat*, the chronological argument, and the
   treatment of the non-composite soul (the paper's Sections 2, 4, and 6) — is my
   own analysis; there Claude acted as an editorial/research assistant, expanding
   prose from my outlines and tightening phrasing. The framing sections — the
   introduction, the "confirmation, not appropriation" discussion, and the
   conclusion (Sections 1, 5, and 7) — were drafted by Claude from my direction and
   outline, then revised by me over several passes.

2. **Editing.** After drafting, I used Claude across several rounds to soften
   overclaiming language, restore a consistent first-person voice, and hedge or
   remove claims that outran the sources.

3. **Citation and quotation verification.** I had Claude fetch the primary Bahá'í
   texts directly (from bahai.org and the Bahá'í Reference Library) and check the
   manuscript's verbatim quotations and load-bearing citations against the actual
   source text. This caught and corrected several concrete errors before submission
   — a quotation misattributed to *Some Answered Questions* that in fact appears in
   *Paris Talks*; two quotations from *The Promulgation of Universal Peace* whose
   closing clauses did not match the source; a placeholder bibliography entry for a
   non-existent anthology, replaced with the real primary sources; and a quoted
   phrase misattributed to *Selections from the Writings of ʻAbdu'l-Bahá*, replaced
   with the passage the argument actually rests on. I reviewed and approved every
   correction.

The paper's central thesis, interpretation, and conclusions are my own, and I take
full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of all claims and citations,
including the AI-assisted portions. A fuller disclosure statement is included as a
declaration section within the manuscript itself, and I have kept a detailed,
item-by-item verification log that I am glad to share as a supporting appendix.

Please let me know if any of this raises concerns, or if you would like the paper
prepared or disclosed differently. I am happy to adjust before submitting.
