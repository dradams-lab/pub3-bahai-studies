\AbdulBaha taught that religion and science are the ``two wings'' of one truth. This paper tests that principle at the level of structure. It argues that the \Bahai metaphysics of \mahabbat---relational love as the foundation of existence---shares a logical structure with relational quantum mechanics and Whitehead's process philosophy: a world constituted by relationship rather than substance. That what sustains relationship is love is the Writings' claim, not physics'. The Writings articulate this structure decades before Whitehead and before experiments on Bell's inequalities. It is offered not as proof of supernatural authority but as documented independent convergence.
